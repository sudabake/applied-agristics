# Applied Agristics 1.3.0

## 中文

- 生物流程整合为 ME 生物培养器，已采样生物棉签、营养试剂和空白生物种子直接生成对应种子，支持 AE 加工样板与四张独立加速卡。
- 更新培养器透明罐体与封闭细胞模型，ME 注魔祭坛采用银灰／紫色配色。
- ME 种子仓加入自动收容、分类过滤与二次确认推出；控制器可以设置新发现作物默认启用。
- 阵列内部种子取消光照、土壤、群系及基座生长条件，端口支持六向放置，所有联网机器自身不占频道。
- 种子转换仅作用于精华作物，先补并行，再转换本批新增余量；普通作物正常收存种子。
- 改善 AE 风格界面、输出方向配置、悬停说明、JEI 种植产物与可拖动结构预览，新增八阶段生物植株和可选 Jade 显示。
- 优化 JDTE 0.6.4.3 温室生物种子的批量收获，保留物种及上游消耗和结算规则。
- 阵列采用独立完整机壳贴图，剔除内部贴合面，清理旧模型、流光、资源与说明。

### 更新存档

本版删除旧细胞培养器、生物祭坛、两级种子仓、空胶囊、成熟培养物、生物萃取物和凝聚生物精华，以及旧投掷／滋养效果。上述内容没有迁移处理，请在升级前处理旧库存并备份存档。客户端与服务器需同步更新。

## English

- Consolidated biological seed production into the ME Biological Cultivator. A sampled Biological Swab, Nutrient Reagent and Empty Biological Seed produce the matching seed. Supports AE processing patterns and four separate acceleration cards.
- Updated the transparent cultivator tank and closed cell meshes, with silver and fluix-purple coloring for the ME Infusion Altar.
- Added automatic seed collection, category filters, confirmed seed ejection and a controller option for enabling newly discovered crops.
- Formed arrays provide their cultivation environment. Crop light, soil, biome and crux requirements no longer gate array production. Ports support six-way placement and machines require no channels themselves.
- Seed conversion applies to essence crops, fills parallel capacity first and converts newly produced surplus only. Ordinary crop seeds retain normal routing.
- Improved AE-style screens, output-side controls, tooltips, JEI product listings and draggable structure previews. Added eight biological crop growth stages and optional Jade information.
- Improved biological seed batch harvesting in JDTE 0.6.4.3 greenhouses while retaining species identity and upstream resource accounting.
- Updated complete casing faces and internal-face culling, and removed obsolete models, effects, resources and instructions.

### Existing worlds

Removed the old Cell Cultivator, Biological Altar, tiered seed stores, capsules, mature cultures, biological extracts, condensed biological essence and the old thrown-culture/nourishment effects. These retired entries have no migration. Handle affected inventories and back up worlds before upgrading. Update clients and servers together.
